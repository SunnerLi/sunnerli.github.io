Researcher

a6214123 (at) gmail (dot) com

I’m a researcher at Chunghwa Telecom in Taoyuan, Taiwan. I’m interested in making deep learning work where textbook assumptions break. Right now, I focus on small, efficient models for on-device AI agents and on keeping those agents secure. I also spent about three years working on generative models.

Before that, I spent five years at MediaTek, first as a computer vision and deep learning research engineer (data-driven RAW denoising for camera ISPs), then as an AI software engineer building LLM tools used in production. I received my M.S. from the Institute of Computer Science and Engineering at National Chiao Tung University, advised by Prof. Yu-Tai Ching and working with Prof. Wei-Chen Chiu and Dr. Li Su, and my B.S. from National Taipei University, where I worked with Prof. Bor-Shing Lin.

Outside of work, I ski ⛷️ and hike 🥾 abroad. See where I’ve been, and where I hope to go, in outside the lab. Feel free to reach out if you’d like to talk research, or if you’re looking for a ski or hiking buddy.
